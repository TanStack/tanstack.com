![TanStack + Kody](/blog-assets/kody-partnership/header.svg)

[Kody](https://kody.codes/) is joining TanStack as a Bronze partner. Their support helps pay for the maintenance behind the libraries we ship.

If you've ever walked a coding agent through a release checklist and then had to teach the next session the same steps, you'll recognize the problem Kody works on. With Kody, you can save a workflow as a package, connect the credentials it needs, and call it again from another agent.

Thanks to Kody for backing our maintainers. You can [read about the partnership](/partners/kody) or [connect your first agent](https://kody.codes/docs/connect-your-agent).
